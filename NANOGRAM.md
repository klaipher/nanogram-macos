# Nanogram for macOS

Nanogram is an unofficial Telegram client for macOS, forked from
[Telegram for macOS](https://github.com/TelegramMessenger/telegram-macos) and kept in sync with it.
It is the Mac sibling of Nanogram for iOS: the same app, tuned to build faster and run lighter.

- Apple Silicon only, macOS 15 or newer, Xcode 27.
- Two editions from one codebase: App Store / TestFlight (sandboxed) and a direct download (Developer ID, notarized,
  Sparkle updates).
- Developer entry point: `tools/dev/tg` (see `docs/local-development.md`).
- Design and plans: `docs/superpowers/specs/`, `docs/superpowers/plans/`.
